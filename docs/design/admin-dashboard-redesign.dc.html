What this tab is for
{{ screenTitle }}
Who is holding the mandal's cash
Each volunteer's balance is the cash they collected, minus what they handed to a treasurer and minus any cash they spent for the mandal. The total at the top is money that exists, but isn't in the box or the bank yet.
Mark settled — when they forgot to log it
If a volunteer hands you cash but never records it, tap Mark settled on their row and record it yourself. Their balance drops, the handover appears in the log, and the books stay balanced.
Fixing a mistake
Tap any handover to void it. Nothing is deleted — the amount simply goes back onto that volunteer's cash-in-hand.
Every rupee leaving the fund
The total at the top is what the mandal has spent, split between the cash box and the bank. Spend by category shows where it went, following whatever filters you set.
Logging a payment
Pick a category — or add your own — write what it was for, the amount, and whether it came from cash or the bank. Cash payments reduce the payer's cash-in-hand automatically.
Fixing a mistake
Tap any expense to edit it, or void it. Voided rows stay in the audit trail but count towards nothing.
What donors and volunteers see
Your name, address, contact number, logo and signature go onto every receipt and onto the public report. Getting these right once is what makes a receipt look official.
Two different switches
Here you choose WHO may open the report. Whether it's published at all lives on the Report tab — so you can prepare quietly and open it up when the mandal is ready.
Nothing saves until you tap Save
The bar at the bottom tells you when there are unsaved changes, so you can edit a few sections and save once.
One page the whole galli can trust
Totals only — what came in, what went out, and what's still in hand. No donor is ever named, and no single expense is listed, so it's safe to share anywhere.
Publishing and unpublishing
Until you publish, only signed-in members of the mandal can open the link. Publishing opens it to anyone with the link, and unpublishing closes it again — the figures keep updating either way.
Sharing it
Copy the link into your society's WhatsApp group. The preview below is exactly what opens on their phone — no login, no app.
Who can do what
Volunteers collect donations and hand cash over. Admins see the whole console — collections, expenses, handovers. The owner is the one admin who can hand the mandal to someone else.
Inviting someone
An invite comes as a link and a code. Send the link on WhatsApp, or read the code out to the person standing in front of you. Both expire in 7 days; resend makes a fresh pair.
Deactivating instead of deleting
A deactivated member can't log anything, but everything they recorded stays in the books — so last season's totals never change. Reactivate them whenever they're back.
One list, two ways to read it
Donations shows every receipt as it was logged. Donors is the same money grouped by person, so a shop that gave three times reads as one donor.
The total always matches the view
Search and filters narrow both views at once, and the figure on top describes exactly what you're looking at — never the whole season unless the filters say so.
Following up with a donor
Tap any row for the receipt, the donor's number, and call or WhatsApp. Deleting a donation hides it from totals and the public report, but keeps the record.
